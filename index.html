About PINY

A practical bridge between global supply and African demand.

PINY Distributions is an emerging distribution company focused on making it easier for international brands to enter and grow in African markets — starting with Kenya.

Our approach is deliberately focused: understand real demand first, build strong supplier relationships, reduce inventory risk, and grow category by category.

We are starting small, learning from the market, and building toward a wider platform for distribution, fulfilment, warehousing and e-commerce across East Africa.

How we are building

Evidence before scale.

PINY is being built around a simple principle: prove demand before committing large amounts of capital to inventory.

01
Listen to the market

Understand what customers actually need, buy and struggle to source.

02
Build supplier relationships

Work with credible international brands and negotiate practical entry models.

03
Validate unit economics

Test landed cost, pricing, margins, lead times and repeat demand.

04
Scale what works

Expand inventory, categories, infrastructure and geography only after proof.
