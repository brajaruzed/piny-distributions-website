About PINY

Helping useful products reach the people who need them.

PINY Distributions is a young Kenyan distribution business. We are starting with real customer conversations, careful costing and small test orders.

Our story

Rooted in Kenya, with an international outlook.

PINY means “world” or “land” in Dholuo. The name reflects our roots and our aim to connect African customers with useful products from around the world.

We are building one category at a time: speak with customers, find reliable suppliers, check the full cost of bringing products into Kenya and test demand before buying more stock.

Over time, we want to add local inventory, warehousing, online ordering and wider distribution across East Africa.
